Intelligent Farming Tools

Everything Needed for Bumper Harvests

Six integrated agronomic modules designed to eliminate farming guesswork and maximize crop profits.

🌱

Agronomic Crop Guides

Comprehensive cultivation manuals for Groundnut, Paddy, Cotton, Chilli, Bengal Gram, and Mango. Seed rates, spacing, fertilizer doses, and irrigation stages.

🌦️

7-Day Spray Weather

Hyperlocal rain risk, wind speed, and humidity tracking powered by Open-Meteo. Know exactly when it is safe to spray chemical pesticides or apply top-dressing fertilizers.

💰

Daily APMC Mandi Rates

Live market prices from Kadapa, Kurnool, Guntur, and Tirupati agricultural markets. Monitor minimum, maximum, and modal bhav to sell at peak market value.

📷

AI Leaf Disease Scanner

Snap a photo of any diseased crop leaf. Our computer vision model identifies Tikka leaf spot, Blast, Wilt, and Leaf curl within seconds with ANGRAU-certified treatment steps.

📅

Stage Growth Calendar

Visual milestone tracking from sowing to harvest. Get automated reminders for basal fertilizer, weeding, vegetative growth, flowering, and pod development.

🏛️

Govt Welfare Schemes

Direct eligibility criteria, passbook requirements, and application guides for YSR Rythu Bharosa (₹13,500/year), PM-KISAN, PMFBY crop insurance, and drip subsidies.
